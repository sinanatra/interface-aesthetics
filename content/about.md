> Interface: A point at which independent systems or diverse groups interact.
> [_The American Heritage Dictionary_](https://www.ahdictionary.com/word/search.html?q=interface)

A person builds an interface, another person uses it. In between, willingly or not, it is decided what is visible, what is possible and who can access it. A key safe, a phone number, a doorknob, a map, a spreadsheet are all interfaces, which we will think of as relationships, rather than objects.
_Interface:_ For [Johanna Drucker](https://attachments.are.na/23491749/923ae476dd101951b2b630d633621fea.pdf), an interface is a dynamic space of relations and a zone of affordances that provokes cognitive experience. Rather than serving as a neutral tool for completing tasks, it mediates what we read through how we read it.
_Aesthetics:_ From the Greek _aisthesis_, what pertains to the senses. [Eyal Weizman and Matthew Fuller](https://attachments.are.na/43821297/84e2b77272ed574edb3e2889ce6d4cb2.pdf) split it in two: sensing, the capacity to register or be affected, which belongs to bodies, materials and machines alike; and sense-making, the capacity to turn what is registered into knowledge. Everything senses, but not everything makes sense. An interface does both.

## Method

Every lesson starts with a question and an object. We look at a few cases, read a text together and argue about it. The pages here are only where the discussion starts.


## Project & Assessment

Working individually or in small groups, you will design a _low-threshold interface_ starting from the [case study](/case-study) (or a real local support network agreed during reviews). Your proposal must be built so that others can understand, use, copy and maintain it without depending on you, in any forms it will entail.

For the exam, you will bring:

1.  The project, presented and discussed with the class;
2. An individual critical text (around 1,500 words, in Italian or English) reading your proposal and the problem it addresses through the references of the course;
3. A discussion on the [readings](/readings).

Evaluation is based on the depth of your analysis, continuity during reviews, how you use the course references, and how accessible, durable and reproducible your proposal is.
