_Interface:_ For [Johanna Drucker](https://attachments.are.na/23491749/923ae476dd101951b2b630d633621fea.pdf), an interface is a dynamic space of relations and a zone of affordances that provokes cognitive experience. Rather than serving as a neutral tool for completing tasks, it mediates what we read through how we read it.
_Aesthetics:_ From the Greek _aisthesis_, what pertains to the senses. [Eyal Weizman and Matthew Fuller](https://attachments.are.na/43821297/84e2b77272ed574edb3e2889ce6d4cb2.pdf) split it in two: sensing, the capacity to register or be affected, which belongs to bodies, materials and machines alike; and sense-making, the capacity to turn what is registered into knowledge. Everything senses, but not everything makes sense.

## Method

Each lesson involves examining a number of case studies, reading texts and analysing them critically.
The texts published on the website are intended to stimulate discussion.

## Project & Assessment

The assignment is to be carried out individually or in small groups, in which you will design a _low-threshold interface_ based on the [case study](/case-study).

For the assessment, you are expected to submit:

1.  The project;
2.  An individual critical essay (approximately 1,500 words, in Italian or English) analysing your proposal and the problem it addresses in the light of the course readings;
3.  A discussion of the [readings](/readings).

Assessment is based on the depth of your analysis, your consistency during revisions, the way in which you apply the course materials, and the extent to which your proposal is accessible, sustainable and reproducible.
