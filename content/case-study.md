# Case Study

## How do we design interfaces that last?

Since 2001, Autistici/Inventati had been hosting email accounts and websites for movements and associations. In August 2026, the United States placed it on a sanctions list and, in September, the collective [suspended every service](https://www.ilfattoquotidiano.it/2026/09/07/autistici-inventati-sanzioni-trump-chiusura-notizie/8498485/).
Platforms and interfaces often depend on just a few people, on funding that runs out or, as in the case of A/I, can shut down from one day to the next. The ones that last are often the simplest, even analogue ones.

![|medium](https://d2w9rnfcy7mm78.cloudfront.net/52829205/original_38caecd905aa18466f54e8a6b2183913.png?1794836855?bc=0)

In Bologna, alongside therapy, there are many activities that can help those going through a difficult time, ranging from sport to cultural programmes. In the field of mental health, these are referred to as ‘social psychiatry’ practices and are sometimes prescribed as part of a treatment programme. They are usually recommended by a professional, and each professional is familiar with only a fraction of them. Two people with the same need may be directed in very different directions, depending on the service and the person they happen to meet.
The city has a [map of community resources](https://sitmappe.comune.bologna.it/RisorseSociali/). Services draw on other directories or create their own, and these are almost always compiled by those providing support, not by those seeking it. [Pirate Care](https://pirate.care/), on the other hand, focuses on horizontal knowledge, what people learn from one another.
This is the common starting point for your course project. Before planning anything, start with what already exists and the points where the system breaks down: map out who is looking for these services, who prescribes or recommends them, who is aware of them and who provides them.

Only after tracing these relationships, decide what shape your answer should take:

- _Categories & language:_ How are activities named and grouped, and who is left out by those words?
- _Medium:_ Does it work better as a paper sheet, a phone number, a dataset, an object, a webpage or something else?
- _Maintenance:_ Who updates it on Monday morning?
